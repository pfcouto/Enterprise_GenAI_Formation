## Centralize and streamline your organization's IT support

Manage incidents and service requests through a flexible, scalable Service Desk tailored to the needs of your operation.

With Level 1 and Level 2 support, centralized contact channels, defined SLAs, and various coverage models, Syone’s Service Desk enables you to resolve issues more quickly and ensure a consistent support experience whether in the office, working remotely, or in hybrid environments.

### When IT teams need more support capacity

As organizations grow and technology environments become more distributed and complex, internal IT teams face an increasing volume of requests, incidents, and support needs. Managing requests across different channels, ensuring defined response times, and escalating more complex situations can significantly increase the operational workload on these teams.

To address these challenges, Syone’s Service Desk centralizes and streamlines support operations, ensuring a single point of contact for users, defined processes, and greater predictability in incident and request management.

## Scope and Capabilities of the Service

Resolution of incidents and service requests by first- and second-line teams.

The first level serves as the initial point of contact, handling the logging, triage, and resolution of requests within its scope.

Requests that require further analysis may be escalated to the second level of support.

Logging, categorizing, prioritizing, tracking, and resolving tickets through structured processes.

This approach allows us to track each request from the initial contact through to its resolution or escalation.

Identification and analysis of the causes of recurring incidents, along with monitoring of measures designed to reduce their recurrence.

Incidents that go beyond the scope of L1 or L2 support are escalated to the appropriate technical teams through defined escalation processes.

This helps ensure that requests reach the right people without losing their context and history.

Clear commitments regarding response time and resolution.

Opening, tracking, and prioritizing requests with manufacturers, operators, software providers, and other technology partners, while centralizing communication until the issue is resolved.

Monitoring key service metrics, SLA compliance levels, ticket volume, and relevant trends to support continuous improvement of operations.

## Coverage Models

The service can be configured according to the operating hours, size, and criticality of the operation.

Support for twelve hours a day, five days a week.

Continuous support, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.

The coverage model is defined during the service design process and aligned with the organization’s operational needs.
