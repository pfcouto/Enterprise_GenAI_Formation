# Syone Celebrating Academic Excellence in Partnership with NOVA IMS

Source: https://www.syone.com/news/syone-celebrating-academic-excellence-in-partnership-with-nova-ims
Section: news
Summary: Syone celebrates over a decade of academic excellence by awarding top students in partnership with NOVA IMS.
Scraped: 2026-10-20

30.11.2023 - Syone · 1 min read

In a testament to its commitment to fostering education and recognizing outstanding talent, Syone proudly continues its annual tradition of awarding prizes to the best students in collaboration with NOVA IMS. This longstanding partnership, which has spanned over a decade, sees Syone standing alongside esteemed organizations such as Banco de Portugal, EDP, Accenture, and more in acknowledging excellence in academic achievements.

This year's awards ceremony, attended by João Liquito Oliveira representing Syone's leader for Outsourcing & Nearshore , marked another milestone in the company's enduring collaboration with NOVA IMS. This partnership, dating back to the days of ISEGI (Instituto Superior de Estatística e Gestão de Informação), has played a pivotal role in the development of a postgraduate course in collaboration with the university and BNP Paribas. Professor Miguel Castro Neto, the current Dean of NOVA IMS, has been a key figure in steering this collaborative initiative. Syone views its academic partnerships as strategic pillars, enabling the early identification of exceptional talent that significantly contributes to the challenging projects undertaken for national and international clients. The company's commitment to skills development is evident through internal Training, Mentoring, and Career Development Support Programs, empowering professionals for rapid growth and success.

"At Syone, we believe in more than just projects; it's a journey of professional and career development," emphasizes João Gomes, CPO at Syone .

For those seeking a workplace where talent is nurtured and recognized as part of a transformative experience, Syone invites individuals to join its dynamic team. This commitment to education and talent development underscores Syone's dedication to creating an environment that fosters continuous growth and excellence.
