Costs vary based on the level of service, infrastructure size, support hours (e.g., 8x5 vs. 24x7), and technologies involved. Syone offers scalable, pay-as-you-go and fixed-price models to help you control IT spending while delivering enterprise-grade service at a lower cost than maintaining an in-house team.

Outsourcing to Syone helps you reduce operational costs, increase system uptime, strengthen security, and gain access to certified experts in platforms like Red Hat, Elastic, Zabbix, and Microsoft. This allows your in-house team to focus on core business initiatives while we ensure stable, secure IT operations in the background.
