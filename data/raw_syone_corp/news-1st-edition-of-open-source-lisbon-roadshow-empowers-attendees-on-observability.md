# 1st Edition of Open Source Lisbon Roadshow Empowers Attendees on Observability

Section: news
Summary: Discover how the Open Source Lisbon Roadshow 2023 highlighted observability's benefits for businesses, featuring expert insights and practical techniques.

Open Source Lisbon, the biggest open source event in Portugal has changed its concept. It's now divided into several events focused on specific themes. The new concept is called Open Source Lisbon Roadshow, the theme of the first edition was Observability and took place at Centro Cultural de Belém on June 6th, 2023. The event successfully shed light on the profound benefits of observability for businesses and delved into the technical aspects of this emerging field.

Organized by Syone, leading open source solutions in Portugal, Open Source Lisbon Roadshow offered attendees a unique opportunity to gain valuable insights from industry experts and discover how observability can revolutionize their understanding of complex systems.

The event featured two engaging streams tailored to different interests. In the first stream, real-life business cases were presented, highlighting the tangible benefits of observability. Attendees had the chance to learn from Luís Neves Dias, Chief Security Officer at Syone, who shared his expertise and experiences in leveraging observability to navigate complex systems effectively.

The second stream provided a deep dive into the technical aspects of observability. Dimitri Marx, SA Partner Lead for EMEA at Elastic, took the stage to deliver a comprehensive session on the intricacies of observability. Ricardo Iorio, DevOps Engineer at Syone, and Allan Hota, Elastic Engineer at Syone, also shared their insights, providing attendees with practical knowledge and techniques to implement observability solutions.

"Navigating the Insights of Complex Systems" was the central theme of the event, highlighting the vital role of observability in aiding businesses to comprehend and optimize intricate systems. Participants gained a deeper understanding of how observability can provide better insights, enabling them to make data-driven decisions and achieve superior operational efficiency.

As the importance of observability continues to grow, events like the Open Source Lisbon Roadshow play a crucial role in fostering collaboration and empowering businesses to harness the power of observability, ultimately leading to enhanced performance and better decision-making in today's complex digital landscape.
