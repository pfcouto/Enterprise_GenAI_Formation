Turnkey Projects

## From idea to deployment, we handle your custom-built IT solution. Fully delivered and fully operational.

Syone takes care of your entire IT project lifecycle. From scoping and design to architecture, development, testing, and delivery, we manage everything to meet your goals and timelines. With over 25 years of experience, we deliver ready-to-use solutions that work from day one.

#### Define scope and requirements

We start by mapping out your goals, business logic, and technical needs. Together, we set clear specs, priorities, and timelines, laying the groundwork for a focused, realistic project plan.

#### Design, build, and test

Our team handles UX/UI, development, and QA. We build your solution from the ground up, validate it through rigorous testing, and make sure it’s stable, secure, and aligned with your expectations.

#### Go live with confidence

Once the solution is approved, we deploy it fully operational, on time, on budget. No handoffs, no unfinished work. Just a ready-to-use product, running from day one.

#### Stay supported post-launch

We provide ongoing support, maintenance, and updates. Whether you need bug fixes, improvements, or future scaling, we stay by your side to keep everything running smoothly.
