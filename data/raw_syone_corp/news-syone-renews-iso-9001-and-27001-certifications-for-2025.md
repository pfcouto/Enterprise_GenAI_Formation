# Syone Renews ISO 9001 and 27001 Certifications for 2025

Syone has once again successfully renewed its ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certifications, marking the fourth consecutive year of compliance and excellence.

This achievement reaffirms the company’s unwavering commitment to delivering high-quality services and upholding the highest standards in information security.

ISO 9001 certification ensures that Syone continues to maintain a robust quality management system (QMS) , with well-defined processes and trained staff that consistently meet customer and regulatory requirements. By prioritizing quality in every aspect of its operations, Syone guarantees superior services to its clients and partners.

The ISO 27001 certification highlights Syone’s proactive approach to information security, demonstrating that the company has implemented stringent measures to manage and mitigate risks associated with data security. This certification confirms that Syone adheres to international best practices for identifying, addressing, and preventing information security threats, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive data.

In 2022, the ISO 27001 standard underwent significant updates, leading to the release of a new version. Syone has successfully transitioned to the ISO 27001:2022 standard, ensuring that its information security management system aligns with the latest global best practices. This transition reinforces the company’s commitment to staying ahead of evolving security challenges and continuously improving its processes.

"Year after year, we challenge ourselves to raise the bar, and renewing these certifications with zero non-conformities once again proves that excellence is embedded in our DNA, the Syone DNA. Our commitment to quality and security is not just about compliance - it’s about delivering real value and trust to our clients and partners," said Syone’s CEO, Eduardo Taborda.

An independent, accredited certification body conducted a comprehensive audit of Syone’s management systems, processes, and procedures. The auditors emphasized that maintaining a record of zero non-conformities, particularly in a renewal audit, is a remarkable achievement that underscores Syone’s dedication to compliance and operational excellence.
