## Powered by People. Backed by Tech. From Portugal to the World.

At Syone, people come first: our clients, our teams, and the communities we work with. We’ve been delivering high-impact tech solutions for over 25 years, combining deep technical expertise with a hands-on, collaborative approach. From Portugal, we support companies in more than 15 countries across Continents.

Whether you're scaling operations, modernizing systems, or driving transformation, our 150+ specialists are here to help you move faster, smarter, and with confidence. We work across industries, from banking and telecom to retail, energy, and the public sector, always adapting to our clients’ realities and building tech that works in the real world.

### What we do

Our mission is to provide innovative, reliable, and high-quality Open-Source solutions. We are committed to continuous growth and development, cultivating a diverse, skilled, and passionate environment that sets the standard for excellence in the IT industry, for clients and employees.

### Where we’re heading

We are determined to be the leading competency centre for Open-Source Technologies. We aim to be a trusted partner and build strong relationships with our clients, enabling their success and driving growth through our expertise and commitment to excellence.
