# Syone Strengthen Ties With Polish-Portuguese Chamber Of Commerce with a Successful Event About Observability

Source: https://www.syone.com/news/syone-strengthen-ties-with-polish-portuguese-chamber-of-commerce-with-a-successful-event-about-observability
Section: news
Summary: Syone and the Polish-Portuguese Chamber of Commerce hosted an Executive Brunch in Warsaw, focusing on observability and its role in cost-effective executive decision-making.
Scraped: 2026-10-20

14.06.2024 - Syone · 1 min read

Syone, leading open source solutions in Portugal, in collaboration with the Polish-Portuguese Chamber of Commerce, organized a successful Executive Brunch at Restauracja Belvedere, in Warsaw, on June 13th. The event brought together industry leaders, experts, and executives representing companies like Alior Bank, Green-Lights, Medicover and many more, to discuss the crucial topic of "Observability: The Key to Cost Reduction in Executive Decision Making."

The event provided a platform for participants to gain valuable insights and exchange ideas regarding the significance of observability in making informed executive decisions. The distinguished panel of experts panel included specialistscomprised representatives from prominent companies such as Jeronimo Martins (Biedronka Holding) and Syone for example.

A highlight of the event was the special guest speaker, João Brás Jorge, President of the Polish-Portuguese Chamber of Commerce and CEO of Eurobank & Bank Millennium. João delivered an engaging address, providing his unique perspective on observability and its impact on executive decision-making processes. The Executive Brunch not only provided a platform for insightful discussions on observability and cost reduction but also fostered a strong connection between the two entities serving as an opportunity to strengthen ties between Syone and PPCC, which facilitates business relations between Poland and Portugal markets.

Reflecting on the successful Executive Brunch and the collaboration between Syone and the Polish-Portuguese Chamber of Commerce, Luis Neves Dias, Chief Strategy Officer of Syone, remarked, "The event served as a milestone in our partnership with the PPCC. It brought together industry leaders and experts from Poland and Portugal, fostering meaningful connections and highlighting the immense potential for collaboration between our two countries."
