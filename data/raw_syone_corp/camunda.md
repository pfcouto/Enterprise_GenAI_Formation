### What we do

#### Replace & Retire

We help you replace homegrown or outdated workflow tools with Camunda’s lightweight, developer-friendly platform. Using Camunda Modeler and BPMN 2.0, we map and design your processes for clarity, standardization, and automation-readiness, delivering scalable systems that are easy to maintain and customize.

#### Modernize & Migrate

Whether you're dealing with legacy BPMS or rigid IT systems, we support your migration to Camunda. Syone configures Camunda’s engine to orchestrate tasks across monoliths, microservices, RPA bots, and more, improving traceability, speed, and integration across your infrastructure.

#### Centralize & Scale

We build centralized automation platforms that provide real-time visibility and control. With Camunda Operate and Optimize, you can monitor live process performance and uncover improvement opportunities, driving continuous optimization backed by actionable analytics.
