Zabbix is an open-source, enterprise-level monitoring platform designed to give organizations full visibility into their IT systems, from on-prem servers to complex, hybrid, cloud-native infrastructures. With its powerful feature set, extensibility, and zero licensing costs, Zabbix has become a go-to solution for businesses seeking to monitor availability, performance, and security in real time.

In this article, we’ll explain what Zabbix is used for , how it works, and how it compares with other monitoring tools.

### Key Takeaways

- Zabbix is an open-source, enterprise-grade platform that monitors networks, servers, virtual machines, containers, applications and cloud services in one place.
- It collects data through its own agents and standard protocols such as SNMP, IPMI, JMX and SSH, and offers a REST API for integrations.
- Typical uses include infrastructure health, service availability, alerting and automated response, dashboards and SLA reporting.
- A distributed architecture with proxies lets Zabbix scale from a few hundred to hundreds of thousands of devices and metrics.
- Zabbix has no licensing costs or feature limits; the real costs are infrastructure, implementation and support.
