## Understanding the importance of nearshore development

Nearshore development involves outsourcing IT services to neighboring or nearby countries, typically within similar time zones. This model offers numerous advantages compared to offshore options, such as easier communication, cultural compatibility, and faster turnaround times.

According to Deloitte's 2022 Global Outsourcing Survey , companies increasingly favor nearshore models to mitigate risks associated with distant outsourcing, highlighting its growing relevance in global IT strategies.

### Time zone compatibility

Effective real-time collaboration hinges on overlapping working hours. Nearshore teams operating within a one to three-hour time difference allow for synchronous communication, daily stand-ups, and quick problem resolution.

This proximity enhances project management, accelerates development cycles, and reduces project risks compared to more distant offshore options.
