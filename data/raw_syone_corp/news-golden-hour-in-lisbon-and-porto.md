# Golden Hour in Lisbon and Porto

Source: https://www.syone.com/news/golden-hour-in-lisbon-and-porto
Section: news
Summary: Syone’s team building in Lisbon and Porto strengthens bonds, boosts culture, and marks the return to work with positive energy and team spirit.
Scraped: 2026-10-20

01.04.2026 - Syone · 1 min read

To mark the end of summer and make the most of the opportunity to bring the team together, Syone organised another successful team building event in Portugal : in Lisbon at Clube Ferroviário and in Porto at Hotel Vincci Porto.

The events brought together all staff members and marked the return to work after the holidays, with the aim of strengthening bonds between teams, promoting company culture, and providing opportunities for sharing.

The day ended with fun and shared experiences, filled with a contagious positive energy that gave the “start of the working year” the best possible boost.

For participants, the most memorable part of the gathering was “getting to know everyone and truly feeling part of the team” , highlighting the importance of team integration and employee experience .
