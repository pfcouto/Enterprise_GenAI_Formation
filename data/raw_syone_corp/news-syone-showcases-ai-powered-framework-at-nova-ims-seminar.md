Syone was invited by NOVA IMS – School of Information Management and Data Science of the Nova University of Lisbon to present their AI-powered framework and successful implementation at Mota-Engil Global at their prestigious Information Systems Seminar.

The Seminar, open to students from all three academic cycles at NOVA IMS and the general public, attracted around 70 participants eager to explore real-world applications of cutting-edge IT solutions, like ezSearch.

Eduardo Taborda, CEO of Syone , led the presentation. He provided an insightful introduction to Syone’s expertise in open-source solutions and its role in delivering digital transformation projects across industries. He then introduced ezSearch , an advanced search and knowledge management framework designed to optimize data accessibility and retrieval .

A key highlight of the session was the Mota-Engil Global success story . Mota-Engil, a key player in the global construction and infrastructure industry, operating in 21 countries , faced challenges related to managing vast amounts of unstructured information . Their existing knowledge management platform struggled with:

- Information overload due to scattered and unclassified data.
- Inefficiencies in data retrieval , leading to duplicated efforts.
- Language barriers impact collaboration across regions.

To overcome these challenges, Syone implemented its AI framework to redefine knowledge access within Mota-Engil Global. With this transformation:

- Over 22,000 documents were successfully indexed and optimized.
- AI-powered search capabilities enabled employees to access relevant information in seconds .
- Multilingual support ensured seamless collaboration across Mota-Engil’s Global workforce.
- User engagement surged , making the knowledge management system a core part of daily operations.

The remarkable results set a new standard for knowledge management in the construction industry.

This session provided an excellent platform for future professionals and business leaders to engage with real-world applications of AI, search technologies, and digital transformation , like ezSearch.

The audience, composed of students and faculty , showed strong interest in ezSearch’s capabilities , leading to a dynamic Q&A session with Eduardo Taborda.

Syone is committed to fostering innovation and collaboration between industry and academia, ensuring that the next generation of IT leaders is equipped with the knowledge and skills to drive future technological advancements.
