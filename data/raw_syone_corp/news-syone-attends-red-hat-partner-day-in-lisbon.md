# Syone Attends Red Hat Partner Day in Lisbon

Lisbon recently hosted one of the most anticipated events in the open-source world, Red Hat Partner Day, and Syone, as Red Hat’s Premiere Partner, was there. With the Tagus River as a stunning backdrop, dozens of Red Hat partners gathered to explore new business opportunities, cutting-edge innovation, and the future of digital transformation.

Recognized as a Competence Center for Open Source technologies , Syone has deep expertise in Red Hat solutions. Our strategic partnership with Red Hat has fueled several mission-critical projects, helping organizations accelerate their digital transformation with technologies like Red Hat OpenShift , Red Hat Ansible , and Red Hat Enterprise Linux .

Throughout the event, Red Hat outlined its key focus areas for 2025: Artificial Intelligence, virtualization, and, most importantly, its Partners. Julia Bernal , Red Hat’s Country Manager for Spain and Portugal, emphasized the need for companies to innovate faster, do more with less, and stay compliant with increasing regulatory demands.

At Syone , we share this vision and deliver it through managed services, turnkey projects, nearshore and outsourcing , and Red Hat-based solutions . "Our long-standing partnership with Red Hat is rooted in a shared vision of innovation, reliability, and open-source excellence. Together, we've delivered countless mission-critical projects that empower our clients to scale, modernize, and lead their industries.", said João Liquito Oliveira , Chief Commercial Officer at Syone.

Another key highlight was the growing relevance of virtualization , with companies needing to preserve their virtual machine workloads while preparing for the future. Sérgio Seabra , Senior Solutions Architect at Red Hat, emphasized that Red Hat OpenShift Virtualization offers a modern, unified virtualization approach with consistent management capabilities .

OpenShift Virtualization enables organizations to plan and execute a fast-paced migration journey for both Linux and Windows virtual machines with reduced risk. Its unified architecture supports all workloads , helping organizations cut costs by adopting a single platform model for development and operations.

The event was not only a platform for keynotes and roundtables but also an invaluable networking opportunity to connect with fellow Red Hat partners and explore new ways to co-create innovative solutions that drive real business value .

As a Red Hat Premier Partner, Syone is at the forefront of open-source innovation. With over 25 years of experience, 500 clients across 15+ countries, and deep technical know-how, we remain committed to transforming businesses through technology.
